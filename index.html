Control and authorization ＋

Funds are controlled by a Solana program with no administrator withdrawal, owner replacement or Falcon bypass. Every authorized operation includes a nonce and an expiry slot.

Creating and activating a vault ＋

Creation has two steps: registering your public key, then activating the vault with proof that you hold the Falcon key. You verify your downloaded recovery file before the first transaction.

Your encrypted recovery file ＋

Your backup uses AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations. The private key is not stored in localStorage or sent to a server. Import it again after closing the window.

Key loss and the rent reserve ＋

Both keys are required. Losing your Falcon key or access to your Phantom wallet prevents withdrawal. The vault has no team-assisted recovery, key rotation or vault closure. The SOL rent reserve remains in the account.

Supported assets ＋

SOL, SPL Token and basic Token-2022 transfers are supported. Transfer hooks, transfer fees and other special token extensions are not supported. Token issuers retain their existing permissions.

What post-quantum means here ＋

Falcon-512 adds a post-quantum signature to this application’s withdrawal permissions. It does not make the entire Solana network quantum-resistant. The code has not been independently audited.
